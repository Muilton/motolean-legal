MotoLean: Corner Coach

Lean angle and corner analysis for motorcyclists. Record your rides, see your lean angle, and review each corner: braking, turn-in, apex and exit.

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: muilton.apps@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Frequently asked questions

The lean angle looks wrong.
Mount the phone firmly, then calibrate in the app while the bike stands upright. A loose mount or a phone in a pocket gives unreliable data.

Recording stops when the screen is off.
Allow location access “While Using the App”, enable background recording in the app’s settings, and avoid Low Power Mode during rides.

Corner scores say “approximate”.
Accuracy depends on how often the GPS reports your position. Use the “Maximum” GPS setting for the best results.

Can I export my rides?
Yes, open a ride and use the share button to export a CSV or GPX file.

Do tips unlock anything?
No. The app is free and has no ads. Tips are an optional way to support development.

Safety

Keep your eyes on the road. The app is a tool for reviewing rides afterwards and is not a safety device. Ride within your abilities and the law.
