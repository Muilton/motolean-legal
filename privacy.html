Privacy Policy

MotoLean: Corner Coach (“the app”) · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

The developer does not collect, receive or share your personal data. Your rides are stored on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app uses Apple services for maps, place names and tips, as described below.

What the app records

When you record a ride, the app uses the following sensors on your device:

  • Location (GPS) — to measure speed, distance and show your route on a map.
  • Motion sensors (gyroscope and accelerometer) — to calculate lean angle and analyze corners.

Rides, routes, statistics, bike profiles in the Garage and app settings are stored on your device. We have no access to them.

Location in the background

Background recording is on by default and can be turned off in the app’s settings. While it is on, the app keeps using your location while the screen is off so that the ride is not interrupted. iOS shows an indicator while this is active. You can stop recording at any time, or turn location off in iOS Settings.

Maps and place names

Maps are provided by Apple Maps. To draw the map and to show the name of the road next to a corner, the app sends the map area you are viewing and the coordinates of that corner to Apple. Apple does not link these requests to your Apple Account. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Files and backups

Your rides are stored in the app’s Documents folder. You can see them in the Files app (On My iPhone → MotoLean), and they are included in the backups of your iPhone that you make to iCloud or to a computer. Deleting the app deletes them from the device.

Sharing and export

Your data leaves the device only if you choose to export or share a ride (for example as a CSV or GPX file) using the iOS share sheet. What happens to the file afterwards is up to you and the app you share it with.

Tips (In-App Purchases)

The app has optional tips for the developer. They are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or any other personal data. Tips do not unlock any features. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the payment: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and use them only to reply. If you send a message from the app, it includes your app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language, so we can troubleshoot. You see the full message before sending it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
